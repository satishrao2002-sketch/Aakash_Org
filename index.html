How centre staffing grows

Staff are added in three stages as enrolment grows. The Centre Manager starts out doing everything.

Under 150 students

Centre Manager does it all

The Centre / Branch Manager handles BD and counselling as well as running the centre. The size is lean and hands-on.

150 – 300 students

Temporary support from India

Counsellors and BD staff travel from India on a temporary basis to support the centre while enrolment grows.

300+ students

Dedicated hires, if needed

A dedicated counsellor or BD executive is hired locally where the country needs one. The need is driven by scale and country rules.

Country differences: UAE needs more staff and dedicated roles sooner. In Kuwait and Qatar, separate counsellor and BD roles are needed only once a centre crosses 300 students.
