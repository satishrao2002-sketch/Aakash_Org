Aakash GCC Organisation Structure

One structure for every centre in every Gulf country, built lean first. At the start, Centre Managers and the Gulf Academic Head report directly to the Business Head – GCC. Country Heads are added as each country grows past 6 centres.

Reporting structure

Stage of growth

Starting structure. Every Centre Manager reports directly to the Business Head – GCC, and so does the Gulf Academic Head. Faculty and operations sit under the Gulf Academic Head.

Once a country has 6 active centres, a Country Head is deployed there, one for every 7–8 centres. That country's Centre Managers then report to the Country Head. Countries below 6 centres stay on the Phase 1 lines.

Country layer

Country roles are added only when a country is large enough to need them. Until then, the Business Head and Gulf Academic Head cover the country directly.

Business

Country Head

Deployed once a country has 6 active centres, one for every 7–8 centres. Owns revenue, admissions and partner relationships, and takes over the Centre Managers in that country. Reports to the Business Head – GCC.

Academic

Country Academic Head

Shown as deployed at the same stage as the Country Head. Owns teaching quality and results, with faculty reporting here. Reports to the Gulf Academic Head.

Operations

Operations Executive

Sits at each centre: timetables, batch set-up, facilities, schedules. Reports to the Gulf Operations Manager, dotted to the Centre Manager.

How centre staffing grows

Staff are added in three stages as enrolment grows. The Centre Manager starts out doing everything.

Under 150 students

Centre Manager does it all

The Centre / Branch Manager handles BD and counselling as well as running the centre. One person does all the start-up work, then moves on to managing the centre.

150 – 300 students

Temporary support from India

Counsellors and BD staff travel from India on a temporary basis to support the Centre Manager through admissions.

300+ students

Dedicated hires, if needed

A dedicated counsellor or BD executive is hired locally where the country needs one. The need varies by country.

Country differences: UAE needs more staff and dedicated roles sooner. In Kuwait and Qatar, separate counsellor and BD roles are needed only once a centre crosses 300 students.

    Confirmed decisions

    1. Lean start: all Centre Managers report directly to the Business Head – GCC.
    2. A Country Head is deployed only once a country has 6 active centres, one for every 7–8 centres.
    3. The Gulf Academic Head reports to the Business Head – GCC, not to the Academic Head in India.
    4. School-integrated programmes have a Programme Coordinator in place of a Centre Manager, with admissions and fees handled by the school.
    5. One faculty set is 4 faculty. At full capacity a set can teach about 270 students, but it is planned for 210–220 students so faculty have time for doubt-clearing sessions.
